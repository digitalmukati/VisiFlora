VisiFlora ingredients: the four formula groups

Below is an original, plain-language map of the nutrients the vendor highlights. Amounts for many botanicals sit inside a proprietary blend on some labels, so always read the Supplement Facts panel on the bottle you receive.

1. Vision Defense Matrix

Astaxanthin

A carotenoid antioxidant used in many vision formulas. The vendor highlights it as a high-potency carotenoid for retinal oxidative stress support.

Vitamin C & Vitamin E

Classic water- and fat-soluble antioxidants. Together they are commonly used to support tissue integrity against everyday oxidative load.

Copper & Selenium

Trace minerals that participate in antioxidant enzymes. Listed here as part of the daily mineral backbone.

Chromium

A trace mineral included for metabolic support in the vendor’s story around long-term visual wellness.

2. Gut-Eye Barrier Complex

Grape seed extract, rutin & quercetin

Bioflavonoids grouped by the brand as a “barrier” trio. They are widely used in circulation and vascular-support botanicals.

Taurine

An amino sulfonic acid found in retinal tissue research. VisiFlora includes it in the gut-eye cluster rather than as a standalone eye drop or probiotic.

Alpha lipoic acid

A dual-soluble antioxidant often described as helping recycle other antioxidants such as vitamins C and E.

3. Vision Performance Boosters

Ginkgo biloba & Coleus forskohlii

Botanicals the vendor links to microcirculation and nutrient delivery. Circulation claims should be read as marketing language, not a medical measurement on every user.

Eyebright

A traditional herbal used in historical European eye-comfort teas and tinctures; here it is inside a capsule blend.

4. Macular & Lens Protection Blend

Vitamin A (beta carotene)

Needed for normal low-light vision pathways. Listed as a disclosed vitamin on typical Supplement Facts panels.

Lutein & zeaxanthin (from marigold)

The two macular carotenoids most shoppers already know from AREDS-style formulas. VisiFlora markets them as internal blue-light filters.

Zinc (11 mg)

A moderate zinc dose compared with some high-zinc eye formulas. Zinc supports retinal enzymes; very high doses can upset the stomach.

Bilberry extract

Anthocyanin-rich berry extract historically associated with night-vision folklore. Modern use is as an antioxidant botanical.

Lycopene

A tomato carotenoid included for lens-oriented antioxidant support.

Saffron extract

A spice extract studied in some visual-acuity nutrition papers. Dose and extract standardization are not always listed outside the blend.
